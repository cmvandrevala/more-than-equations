## 🎯 Problem Statement

Write a program that prints out the following values to the console:

1. A random integer in the range of 0 to 50.
1. A random integer in the range of 10 to 100.
1. A random integer in the range of -8 to 0.
1. A random integer in the range of -23 to -9.
1. A random integer in the range of -40 to 40.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

* Be sure to seed your program correctly. You should see a different set of numbers every time you run the program!
