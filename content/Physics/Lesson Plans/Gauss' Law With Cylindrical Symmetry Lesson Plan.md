- Cylindrical symmetry

## 🎯 Objectives

- Utilize the different charge distributions on conductors and insulators to calculate electric fields
- Calculate the electric field from cylindrical charge distributions using Gauss' law

- 🟢 Cylindrical symmetry
- 🟣 Electric field around a line of charge

- 🟣 Electric field near the surface of a conductor
