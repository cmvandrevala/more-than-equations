## 🔖 Topics

- Gauss' law
- Spherical symmetry
- Points of charge
- Balls of charge
- Shells of charge

## 🎯 Objectives

- Utilize the different charge distributions on conductors and insulators to calculate electric fields
- Calculate the electric field from spherical charge distributions using Gauss' law

## 📋 Sequence

- 🟢 Gauss' law
- 🟢 Spherical symmetry
- 🟣 Electric field around a point charge
- 🟣 Electric field around an insulating ball of charge
- 🟣 Electric field around an conducting ball of charge
- 🟣 Electric field around an insulating shell of charge
- 🟣 Electric field around an conducting shell of charge
- 🟣 Electric field around concentric shells of charge
