## 🔖 Topics

- Magnetic force on a current-carrying wire

## 🎯 Objectives

- Solve for the magnetic forces acting on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field
- Solve for the direction of the forces on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field using the right-hand rules

## 📋 Sequence

- 🟣 Net magnetic force on a length of wire
- 🟣 Two parallel wires
- 🟣 Net magnetic force on a loop of wire
- 🟣 Wire suspended in a magnetic field

## 📝 Practice Problems

1. A 20 cm by 30 cm rectangular loop of wire carries 1.0 A of current clockwise around the loop. Find the magnetic force on each side of the loop if the magnetic field is 2.5 T in a direction parallel to the area vector of the loop. What is the net magnetic force on the loop?

## ✅ Partial Solutions

1. Top and bottom: 0.75 N; Left and right: 0.5 N; There is no net magnetic force.
