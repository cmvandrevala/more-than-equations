## 🔖 Topics

- Coulomb's law for a continuous charge distributions

## 🎯 Objectives

- Calculate the electric field from a continuous charge distribution using Coulomb's law

## 📋 Sequence

- 🟣 Along the axis of a ring of charge
- 🟣 At the center of a ring of charge
- 🟣 Simple harmonic motion close to the center of a ring of charge
- 🟣 Above the center of a finite line of charge
- 🟣 Along the axis of a finite line of charge
- 🟣 Above an infinite line of charge
- 🟣 Above a plane of charge
