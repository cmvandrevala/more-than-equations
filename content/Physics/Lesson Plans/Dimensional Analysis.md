## 📝 Practice Problems

1. After experimenting in the lab, I have determined a potential equation that relates the amount of liquid flowing in a pipe to the flow and pipe area. Use dimensional analysis to determine if the following equation could be valid:

$$
V = C J A^2
$$

- Volumetric Flow Rate (V) = Volume Per Unit Time
- Flow Velocity (J) = Length Covered by Liquid Per Unit Time
- Cross-Sectional Vector Area (A) = Cross-Sectional Area of the Pipe
