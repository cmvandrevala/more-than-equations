## 🔖 Topics

- Torque on a current-carrying loop of wire in a uniform magnetic field
- Potential energy of a current carrying loop of wire in a uniform magnetic field
- Magnetic dipole moment

## 🎯 Objectives

- Calculate the net force acting on a current-carrying loop in a uniform magnetic field
- Calculate the torque acting on a current-carrying loop in a uniform magnetic field
- Calculate the potential energy of a current-carrying loop in a uniform magnetic field
- Compare and constrast magnetic vs. electric dipoles

## 📋 Sequence

- 🟣 Net magnetic force on a loop of wire in a uniform magnetic field
- 🟣 Torque on a loop of wire in a uniform magnetic field
- 🟢 Magnetic dipole moment
- 🟣 Potential energy of a loop of wire in a uniform magnetic field
